# Repository publishing rules

This repository is public. Keep marketing assets and drafts, campaigns, audience or email plans, competitive research, and internal planning or execution documents local. Do not commit or upload them to GitHub, including PR descriptions, comments, or attachments.

Store new private material outside the repository or in the ignored `marketing/`, `internal/`, or `docs/internal/` directories. Existing locally retained documents covered by `.gitignore` must remain untracked. Commit application code, migrations, tests, and public product or technical documentation as needed. Check staged files before every commit for private material.
