Getting in

The owner signs in, picks an aircraft, or adds one. Today the hangar opens on fleet tiles and an amber disclaimer, enrolling promises three logbooks and makes five, and setup asks for the meters twice across a three-step wizard with no way back. Here each aircraft card wears its own paint and says its verdict in a sentence, adding an aircraft shows exactly what it creates, and setup is one screen that ends in a first answer.

What is due

The owner looks at what the records show is coming due and deals with it. Today that answer is split across Status, Forecast and AD / SB, the status strip disappears below 1536px, and every one of those pages is read-only, so marking an oil change done means a trip to another tab. Here a sentence comes first, one list holds all eleven items with both of their limits, and every row has its own Mark done.

Squawks

Something felt wrong in flight and the owner wants it written down and in front of the mechanic. Today a squawk is a line of text with Low, Medium or High; resolving it takes one click with no note, no date and no link to the work that fixed it. Here a squawk carries a photo, says what the records already know about that part, goes to the mechanic as a link, and closes only against the entry that cleared it.

From paper to index

The owner turns photographs of paper pages into confirmed entries. Today that is four screens with no hand-offs: Upload and Capture are dead ends, reading is a separate button bound to an open tab with no progress or allowance shown, and review is split across two screens with different powers and no keyboard. Here one queue shows every page from phone to confirmed, reading runs in the background against a visible daily allowance, and review is one desk that walks the owner from the least certain field to the next page.
