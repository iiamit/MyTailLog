A glance

Dana checks the aircraft from the kitchen or the ramp. Today the phone's home is a list of tiles with no sense of when things fall, and reminders say only that something is due. Here the sentence comes first, then the line ahead: the next year drawn across the width of the phone, with what is due as rings on each book's line and the visit that would cover them drawn where it would happen. The same strip shrinks into a lock-screen widget.

The map

Logbooks opens on the route map: one column per book, newest at the top, so scrolling down is going back in time. Today the phone shows a flat list of books and a timeline that lists entries without showing gaps or which visits signed several books. Here a stop opens its entry and page in a sheet that leaves the map in view, an interchange opens every page of that visit, and a dashed gap says what the other books know about those years.

Planning

Before booking the shop, Dana wants to know what will have lapsed by a given day and what can be done in one trip. Today that means Forecast on one screen and Pick a date on another, with neither showing which items could share a visit. Here the line ahead carries a date scrubber: drag it, and every ring it passes turns solid if the item will have lapsed by then. Suggested visits are dashed interchanges; open one to see what rides along and book it.

After a flight

Back at the tie-down, Dana photographs the tach and checks the number. Today the save says nothing about what changed. Direction B already lists what the flight moved; D also draws it: the oil ring slides nearer on the engine line as the reading goes up, and after the save the line ahead shows that the oil change now falls before the planned visit.

Scanning

On Sunday Dana scans the first airframe pages found for the 1980s. Today scanning spans four screens that do not lead to each other, and nothing says where the new pages belong in the aircraft's life. Here every page lands on its line as it is read: hollow stops while they wait for review, solid once confirmed, and the dashed gap shortens as its pages come in. The review screen carries a small spine so the owner knows which year they are reading.

Finding

Search and Ask are one field. Today an answer arrives as text with a page reference and no sense of when it happened relative to everything else. Here the matching entries light up as stops on a strip of the whole life, so the answer shows where in 48 years it sits as well as which page proves it.

Sharing

Share starts from who will read it. Today the phone can only send a text summary. Direction B builds a record room per audience; D gives that record room the map as its cover, so a buyer or an IA sees the aircraft's whole life, gaps and overhauls included, before opening a tab.

Tablet

On a tablet the binder opens flat: the coloured rail on the left, two leaves side by side, and the spine under every board, as on the web. Today the iPad is a stretched phone in places. Here Status shows the line ahead above the due list with the page beside it, the map gets a right leaf for the pages of whatever is selected, and Plan ahead puts the scrubber and the list side by side.
