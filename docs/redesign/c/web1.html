Line Diagram on the web: status, capture, review

Eighteen desk screens, from signing in to confirming a scanned page. Every screen keeps the four logbook lines in a strip under the top bar, so the owner always sees where in the aircraft's life the thing in front of them sits.

Getting in

The owner signs in, picks an aircraft or adds one, and gets a first answer. Today the hangar leads with a demo aircraft and an amber disclaimer, enrolling promises three logbooks and creates five, and setup asks for the meters twice across three steps with no way back. Here the hangar gives each aircraft its sentence and its line, adding an aircraft draws the five lines it will create, and setup is one screen that ends in the first verdict.

What is due

The owner checks what the records show is coming and deals with it. Today that answer is spread over Status, Forecast and AD / SB, the status strip disappears below 1536px, and every one of those pages is read-only, so marking an oil change done is a trip to another tab. Here the home screen says it in a sentence, the due board is a departures board of all eleven stops in arrival order with both limits on every row, and Mark done works where the item is listed.

Squawks

The owner notes something wrong with the aircraft and gets it to the mechanic. Today a squawk is one line of text with Low, Medium or High, it resolves with one click and no note, and it never appears on the overview. Here a squawk carries a photo and who saw it, can be sent to the mechanic as a link that shows only that squawk, and is closed by pointing at the logbook entry that fixed it.

From paper to index

The owner turns paper pages into confirmed entries. Today that is four screens with no hand-offs: capture and upload are dead ends, reading is a separate manual step that dies if the tab closes, the AI allowance is hidden on the profile page, and review is split between a per-page screen and a bulk screen with different powers. Here it is one pipeline drawn as one line, with the queue and today's allowance always visible, a single review desk that does everything, and a list that confirms the clean pages in one go.
