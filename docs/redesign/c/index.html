The idea

An aircraft's records are a story told across decades and several books at once. Direction C draws them as a transit diagram. Each logbook is a coloured line; each entry is a stop; an annual that signed the airframe, engine and propeller books is an interchange. A stretch with no pages is a dashed line, so gaps are impossible to miss. The lines continue past today, dotted, and the rings ahead are what is due.

Below the diagram, what is due reads like a departures board: one row per stop, in the order they arrive, with fixed columns for when, what, which line, what is left and the status. On the desk the diagram sits on midnight enamel; at the ramp the phone turns porcelain for sunlight.

Best for
Owners who think in history, buyers' mechanics doing research, anyone exploring fifty years of paper.
Density
High. Compact rows, condensed labels, the diagram as a strip on every screen.
Signature
The network diagram and its vocabulary: lines, stops, interchanges, rings, the board.
Honest risks
The most novel, so the first visit needs a short legend. It is the most work to build: the diagram needs a layout engine. Line colours must never be read as status, so status always uses a shape and a word. The analysis argues against dark-only, so C ships both themes and defaults the phone to day.

Encode Sans is the only face. Its width axis does the work a second typeface would: semi-condensed for labels, table heads and diagram names, normal width for reading. Numbers use tabular figures; there is no monospace.

Line inks belong to logbooks (Airframe blue, Engine magenta, Propeller porcelain, Avionics violet) and tag everything from a book: chips, citations, rows. Status uses its own shapes: a filled dot on time, a ring due soon, a square overdue, a dashed ring with no record.

Navigation

Web: a slim rail (Network, Due board, Books, Aircraft, Share, with Settings at the foot) and a command field across the top for search and questions, with Add beside it. Every screen carries a compact strip of the diagram so the owner always knows where in time they are. Phone: Network, Due board, a centred Add, Books and More. The mapping from today's fifteen items is on the overview page.

What changes in the workflows

  • Browsing is travelling. Click a stop to open the entry; drag across a stretch to filter the list to those years.
  • Due is a board, in arrival order, mixing dates and hours on one timeline.
  • Scanning lays track. New pages extend a line; a gap fills in as its pages are confirmed.
  • The dossier is a line map of the aircraft's life with every gap labelled, followed by the board and the citations.
