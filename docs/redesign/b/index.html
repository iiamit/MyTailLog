Direction B: Section Tabs

The records binder every owner already keeps, made into the app. Each section has a coloured tab sized by how much it holds, the open section's colour floods the screen, and every fact sits on a leaf beside the scanned page that proves it.

The idea

Before an annual or a sale, owners build a binder: tabbed dividers, the logbooks, the 337s, the W&B. Direction B is that binder. A dark cover rail holds one coloured tab per section, each tab as tall as the section is full, so the shape of the rail shows where the records are thin. Opening a section floods the screen with its colour; the work happens on white leaves laid over it.

Almost every screen is a spread: the record on the left leaf, the scanned page that proves it on the right, on a dark mat. A due item, an AD, an answer from Ask: the page is already open beside it. Text transcribed from paper is set in a book face, the app's own words in a sans, so the owner can always tell what the logbook says from what the software concluded.

Best for
Desk-first owners, the annual with an IA, pre-buys and audits; the strongest at proving things.
Density
Medium to high. Document-like tables with headings in the margin.
Signature
The tab rail and the record-beside-source spread.
Honest risks
Six section colours could read as playful if used anywhere but the tabs and the board. The spread needs width, so phones show the page as a second step. A full-colour board over a long session may tire; leaves stay white so the working surface is calm.

Libre Franklin is the app's voice, at one 16px body size; headings hang in the left margin of each leaf. Literata is the paper's voice and appears only for words transcribed from a scan: entry narratives, quotes in answers, citations in documents.

No green and no amber anywhere. Current is a black dot; due soon is a vermilion ring; overdue is a solid vermilion slip; unknown is a dashed ring. It reads for colour-blind owners and prints correctly in black and white. Trust shows as a dotted dot (read by AI), a filled dot (confirmed by you) or a diamond (signed by a mechanic).

Navigation

Web: six tabs (Status, Logbooks, Maintenance, Aircraft, Documents, Share) with Search-or-ask, Add and the account at the foot of the rail; sub-tabs sit along the top of the board. Phone: five coloured tabs along the bottom (Documents moves under Aircraft) and a square Add button. The mapping from today's fifteen items is on the overview page.

What changes in the workflows

  • Every list row opens its page. Selecting a due item, an AD or an entry turns the right leaf to the page that backs it, with the entry outlined.
  • Review is the same spread with the leaves swapped in purpose: the page on the mat, the fields on the leaf, confirmed one at a time.
  • Hand-offs are tabbed documents. The dossier and summary print as a binder with dividers, every fact footnoted to its page.
  • Coverage is visible from the rail: a thin Logbooks tab means thin records.
