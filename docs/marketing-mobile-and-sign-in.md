# MyTailLog mobile and sign-in update — October 2026

Copy for the website's owned channels. Check each store listing before posting elsewhere.

## Short announcement

MyTailLog is now available for Android on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mytaillog.app), alongside the [iPhone and iPad app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mytaillog/id6795396758). Scan logbook pages, review your aircraft records, and keep synced scans and documents with you when the hangar has no signal. The apps are free and use the same account as the website.

The website now lets you sign up or sign in with Google or Apple, as well as an email link or password. Already have an account? Sign in with email first and connect Google or Apple in **Profile → How you sign in**. That keeps the aircraft and records you already entered in the same account. [Step-by-step help](https://mytaillog.com/help#profile).

Your paper logbooks remain the legal maintenance record. MyTailLog helps you find and review their contents; confirm anything important against the original page.

## Short social post

MyTailLog is now on Android: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mytaillog.app). The [iPhone/iPad app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mytaillog/id6795396758) is there too. Both let you take synced aircraft records into a hangar with no signal.

On the web, you can now sign in with Google or Apple. If you already use MyTailLog, sign in with email first and connect the new method in Profile so your existing aircraft stay with your account. Free and open source: [mytaillog.com](https://mytaillog.com).

- The public iOS App Store listing still shows version 1.5 as of October 16. Mention Apple sign-in on the website; do not claim it is in the public iOS app until the updated build appears in the store.
