What this fixes

The old screen asked which aircraft — from inside an aircraft context whose header already carried the tail number. The answer was on screen while the question was being asked. Here the aircraft is inherited and shown as trailing metadata, like every other screen in the direction.

It opened as a ‹ Back stack screen, which is the structure this redesign removed. As a sheet it does one job and hands you back the grid you launched it from, so shooting a second logbook means changing a chip rather than navigating out and in again.

It said “queued”, “pending” and “routes to vision extraction” — our words for our machinery. What the owner is choosing is whether the pages have handwriting on them, and what it costs them is time; the copy now says that. The offline queue is described (“saved on your phone”, “upload on the next sync”) and never named.

The 📷 emoji in the primary button becomes camera.viewfinder, as screen 7 already called for. The sentence beneath it exists to say the thing nobody currently knows: the whole stack can be shot in one session, up to 24 pages, without reopening the camera.

“Adds to the end — 24 pages so far” is the only new idea on the screen. Page order is a real concern for a logbook and re-sequencing lives on the web; saying where the pages land stops anyone believing they have to shoot in sequence.
