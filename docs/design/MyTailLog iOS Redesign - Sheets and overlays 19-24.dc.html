What this fixes

The current app can add a reading, an oil top-up, a squawk and a mark-done — and nothing else. Every edit, every correction, every resolution sends the owner to the web. These surfaces close that gap without inventing a second design language: each is the sheet idiom from screens 5 and 11 at compact width and an overlay on the right pane at regular width.

The field editor shows the raw transcription and the crop beneath the value, so the owner corrects against the page rather than from memory. The chip says Uncertain read or Read clearly — never a percentage and never the word "confidence", which is ours. Meters get steppers; the step is 0.1 because that is the resolution of the instrument.

The meter-replacement prompt catches the one entry that breaks every hour-based countdown: a reading lower than the last. It asks the question in the owner's words and explains what saying yes does. "I mistyped it" is the more common answer and is one tap.

Conflicts get a screen, not a toast. Silent last-write-wins is how a logbook loses a signature. Yours and theirs sit side by side with only the differing fields highlighted, and "Decide later" is always available — a conflict is never resolved by dismissing it.

Ask at regular width is a pane over any tab, because "ask while looking at the book" is the iPad-only value. Every answer cites the entry it came from, and the allowance is on the composer, not buried in a profile.
