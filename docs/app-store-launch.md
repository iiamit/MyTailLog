## LinkedIn

Apple approved MyTailLog for the App Store. It's live for iPhone and iPad.

I built it for the aircraft records that tend to live in a stack of paper logbooks. Scan the pages, search the history, and keep the records available when the hangar has no signal (which is usually when you need them).

The app lets you check maintenance status and record meter readings, oil and squawks. Sync once before heading out; offline updates upload on the next sync.

Free and open source. Your paper logbooks are still the legal record. This is the companion that helps you find what's in them.

Aircraft owners and pilots: give it a try and tell me what gets in your way.
