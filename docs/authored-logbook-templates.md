# Authored maintenance-entry templates

The four preventive-maintenance drafts below were approved for the first release. They are **editable entry aids**, not FAA-prescribed sentences or a determination that a given person may perform the work on a given aircraft. The signer supplies actual work, parts, findings, maintenance-data reference, date, and certificate. No template pre-asserts an inspection result or airworthiness.

Sources: [14 CFR 43.9](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-43/section-43.9), [Appendix A(c) to Part 43](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-43/appendix-Appendix%20A%20to%20Part%2043), [FAA AC 43-12A Change 1](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_43-12A_CHG_1.pdf), and [FAA AC 43-9D](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_43-9D.pdf). Required annual and 100-hour inspections use [14 CFR 43.11](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-43/section-43.11) and are excluded.

| Template | Source item | Required details | Generated wording pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oil and filter service | Appendix A(c)(23) covers oil filter elements | Oil brand/type, quantity, filter brand/model/part number, removed-filter findings, instructions/revision, checks | “Drained engine oil and replaced oil filter element with [filter]. Added [quantity] of [oil]. Removed filter findings: [findings]. Work performed in accordance with [instructions]. Checks performed: [checks].” |
| Tire replacement | Appendix A(c)(1) | Wheel position, tire details, pressure, instructions/revision, checks; optional tube | “Replaced [position] tire with [tire] [and tube]. Inflated to [pressure]. Work performed in accordance with [instructions]. Checks performed: [checks].” |
| Spark-plug service | Appendix A(c)(20) | Plugs and part numbers, gap, instructions/revision, checks | “Serviced spark plugs: [plugs]. Set gap to [gap]. Work performed in accordance with [instructions]. Checks performed: [checks].” |
| Battery service | Appendix A(c)(24) | Actual work, battery details, instructions/revision, checks | “[work]. Battery: [battery]. Work performed in accordance with [instructions]. Checks performed: [checks].” |

**Oil eligibility note:** Appendix A(c)(23) explicitly mentions cleaning or replacing oil strainers or filter elements. It does not expressly list an oil-only change. The app therefore offers an oil **and filter** template and tells the signer to confirm that the whole task is eligible for their aircraft and certificate. It does not offer an oil-only pilot template. [FAA AC 43-12A](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_43-12A_CHG_1.pdf) cautions that a listed task may still be ineligible on a particular aircraft if it involves complex assembly.

Existing local test fixtures use short phrases such as “Change oil and filter” and record oil quantity/type and filter findings. They helped keep the generated prose concise. Production log entries were not available to this checkout; no customer data was copied into the templates.

The signer may edit the generated wording before requesting an email signing code. The code is bound to the exact entry values. The signed record stores the final wording, structured answers, signer/certificate snapshot, timestamp, and SHA-256 digest. A database trigger blocks edits and deletion; a correction is a new signed entry linked to the earlier one. The earlier record remains visible for audit and is excluded from current-meter calculations. The digest can be used to detect changes to the stored snapshot, while the email challenge and authenticated session bind the act of signing to the account. This is an electronic-signature workflow, not a personal PKI certificate.

## Printed sticker rescans

Apply `0062_signed_entry_scan_links.sql` before deploying the rescan-aware web pipeline. New stickers print the complete entry ID and digest. Earlier stickers with eight-character IDs and twelve-character digests are recognized too. A matching scan links the physical page to the existing immutable signed entry; it does not create another entry. The review page and signed-entry page show the link. Entries already in that aircraft logbook are skipped when their date, readings, and work text strongly match the new scan. Uncertain matches remain unconfirmed for review, since dropping a distinct maintenance record would be worse than asking the owner to resolve a possible duplicate. Android and iOS use the same server extraction endpoint and need no native rebuild for this behavior.
